Bar

One full-width bar per monitor: a plain 34 px rectangle flush with the top edge and both sides, with no margins, rounded corners or shadow. Workspaces sit on the left, the focused window’s title in the middle, and status and clocks on the right. Shown here at 1536 px wide, a typical laptop, where the zone clocks would leave the title under 200 px, so only the local clock shows and the zone clocks are in its popover. A wider monitor shows all four clocks (see Clocks below); the title stays centered and is cut short when it doesn’t fit.

Left to right: workspaces 1–9, each showing the icons of its windows, and the current workspace's layout symbol (|M| = three-column). Right: third-party tray icons (StatusNotifierItem), then the built-in status icons (keep awake, faint while off; Bluetooth; network; volume, battery and CPU, each with its percentage; notifications; session), then the clocks: local alone here, all four where there’s room. The zone clocks carry short place labels (SF, NYC, LON), so nothing changes at DST; the clocks popover adds each zone’s current abbreviation (PDT/PST, EDT/EST, BST/GMT). The local clock swaps its label for the date.

Workspace states

Occupied: one icon per window, up to 5, then “+N”.

Shown on another monitor: accent outline.
7
Empty: faint number. All nine are always shown.
4chat
Urgent: amber, with the window that asked (or sent a notification) ringed. Clears when that window is focused. Super+Tab jumps to it.

Status states

screen_shareSharingmic
Screen shared / mic live. The red pill shows while a PipeWire screencast stream has a consumer; during a screen or region share, popups are held until it ends.
volume_off64%
Muted: the icon changes and the level dims, showing what unmuting brings back.
notifications_paused
Do not disturb. The icon changes; held popups go to the history.
light_mode
Keeping awake: always in the bar, faint while off and lit while on. A click turns it on for 2 h, or off.
battery_1_bar9%
Battery low: red below 15%, with a critical notification at 7%.
computer87%
CPU hot: amber when the CPU is hot or memory is 90% full, red while it throttles. A click shows the top processes, memory and temperature.
wifi_off
Offline. A VPN adds a small lock to the network icon.
