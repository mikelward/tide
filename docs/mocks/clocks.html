Clocks

Clicking any clock opens one popover: every zone with its offset from local, a 24-hour strip (night / day / 09–17 working hours) with a “now” line, the next DST change, and a month calendar with ISO week numbers. Scrolling over a clock scrubs time forward and back, so all the clocks answer “what time is 3pm Pacific here?”.
