Layouts

dwm/Krohnkite-style master-and-stack layouts, never binary-space partitioning. The layout belongs to the workspace, and the bar shows its symbol. New windows join the stack, and the master stays put. The only focus cue is that unfocused windows are darkened by 7%: no borders and no gaps. The hairlines between windows are just where one app's content meets the next.

How three-column fills up (ultrawide)

Each step is the same layout, so adding or closing a window never reshuffles the ones you were looking at. With one window the single-window rule applies. With two, it's master plus one column. From three on, the master is centered and further windows alternate right, left, right into the side stacks. Super+\ / Super+/ grow or shrink the master (step 2.5%), Super+= / Super+− add or remove a master, and Alt+Tab swaps the focused window into the master.
