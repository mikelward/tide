Picker

Super+C opens the picker on the focused monitor, drawn like the switcher: a Clipboard tab of what you've copied, newest first, and a Characters tab of emoji and symbols, found by name. Tab moves between them; Enter copies the selection and closes, and Ctrl+V pastes it. Every entry here is a placeholder.
