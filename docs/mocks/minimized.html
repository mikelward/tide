Minimized windows: five options

The same desktop each time: workspace re, a terminal and a browser tiled, and two windows minimized from it (Chat and Mail). Each option is what shows while this workspace has minimized windows. A workspace with none shows nothing. In every option, the switcher’s Windows tab also lists the minimized windows, and Enter restores one.

A · Full-width bar along the bottom (the to-do as written)

ShowsEvery minimized title at a glance, as a taskbar does.
CostsA 30 px strip on every monitor, which the tiles shrink to make room for. They re-tile when the first window is minimized and again when the last is restored.
DoableYes on both. On tide it's a second layer-shell bar; on tilemo, an hs.canvas strip plus a smaller tiling area.

B · Floating chip, bottom center

Left: at rest, the minimized apps' icons and a count. Right: clicked, or hovered for half a second, it opens upward into the list. A click on a row restores that window.

ShowsThat something is minimized, and which apps, at 32 px tall and about 140 px wide.
CostsIt floats over the bottom of whichever tile is under it, with no re-tile. It could step aside while the pointer is near it, or sit in the bottom-right instead.
DoableYes. On tide it's a small layer-shell surface with no exclusive zone. On tilemo it's an hs.canvas at a floating level that takes clicks.

C · Floating icons, bottom-right corner (chosen)

One icon per minimized window, like a small tray; hovering one shows its title (shown here over Mail).

ShowsEach window as its own target, with one click to restore.
CostsAbout as small as B, but two windows of one app look alike until you hover, and it grows wide with many windows.
DoableYes, the same way as B.

D · An icon in the top bar

A status icon with the count, beside the others on the right. A click opens the list as a popover from the bar.

ShowsOnly a count. It's the quietest option and covers nothing.
CostsIt's easy to forget a window is down there, and it sits far from where you minimized it.
DoableYes, trivially. It's one more icon in tide's bar and in tilemo's.

E · In the workspace's own chip

The bar already shows each workspace's app icons. Minimized ones show hollow and faded after the rest, and a click on one restores it. A click on the chip still switches to the workspace.

ShowsMinimized windows on every workspace, not just this one, with no new UI.
CostsIt's subtle, and widens the chip a little. Telling two windows of one app apart needs a hover.
DoableYes. It's a change to the existing bar on both.

Chosen: C, floating icons in the bottom-right corner (SPEC.md §6.7). The bar's chips also dropped their app icons, keeping only those of windows that want attention (§7.2), so E's place for them is gone. This page keeps all five as the record; the bars drawn here still show the old chips. The suggestion was E, plus B's popover for the list. It costs no screen space and covers nothing, and it puts the cue where you already look for what's on a workspace. B is the pick if E is too subtle in practice. All five are easy to change later; only A changes the tiling.
