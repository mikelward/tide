Switcher

One overlay with five tabs: Apps (the launcher), Workspaces, Windows, Attention and Actions. Each key opens it on its own tab: a tap of Super on Workspaces, Super+Space on Apps, Super+A on Attention. Tab and Shift+Tab move between the tabs, typing filters the one you're on, and Esc or the same key again closes it. It opens centered near the top of the focused monitor and dims everything behind it.

Apps (Super+Space): the launcher. With no query, the apps you use most and most recently, then the rest, Tide Settings among them; a query is fuzzy-matched across apps, settings pages and actions, its top hit preselected. The key hints teach the bindings. Ctrl+↵ runs the app on a new workspace, asking its name first, and PgDn jumps to the next section.

Windows: every window on any workspace, the one used last first, with its app and workspace. ↵ goes to it, switching workspace. A minimized window is listed under the workspace it came from, marked minimized, and ↵ restores it there. Ctrl+M, or the bell on a hovered row, mutes the row's app; a muted row keeps its bell. Browser tabs join this list later (§8.3).

Actions (Tab from Attention): every quick action, in its own order (screenshots, then the session, then the toggles), with suspend, restart, do not disturb, dark style and clear clipboard further down. Typing filters it. A power action asks only if something blocks it.

Quick actions (built in)

Screenshot window · Screenshot screen · Screenshot region · Lock · Log out · Suspend · Restart · Shut down · Do not disturb (toggle) · Keep awake (toggle) · Presenter mode (toggle) · Dark / light / automatic theme · Clear clipboard history · Reload shell. Tide Settings and each of its pages are listed as apps, not here. A power action asks for confirmation only if unsaved-work inhibitors are active (systemd-inhibit --list).
