tide is the only org.freedesktop.Notifications server. Popups stack under the bar at the top right of the focused monitor, newest on top, at most three at a time, and the rest wait their turn. Clicking a popup runs its default action and focuses the window that sent it, switching workspace if needed.

Popups. Low urgency: 4 s. Normal: 6 s. Critical stays until dismissed. A system critical (battery, polkit) gets through Do not disturb, but a browser one doesn’t, since Chrome marks ordinary reminders critical. Hovering pauses the timer. Action buttons, inline reply (when the sender offers it) and images come from the spec. The volume/brightness OSD is drawn by the shell itself, not sent as a notification, so it never lands in the history.

Notification center: click the bell or press Super+Shift+N. History is grouped by app and survives a shell restart. While a screen or region is shared, popups are held automatically (a window share leaves them alone) and the “held” banner counts them. The media card appears only when an MPRIS player is active.
