From power on to the screensaver

Every screen tide shows, in order. Steps 1–3 are the boot splash (Plymouth, installed by tide-setup --splash); 4 is the greeter (greetd, installed by --greeter); 5 is the session’s own splash; 7 and 8 are the lock. The black of steps 1–3 runs into the greeter’s first frame, which is black too.

1 · Boot
tide
From the initramfs on: tide’s logo and name, and the spinner. The firmware’s own logo shows before this, if it has one.

3 · Boot, then black
tide
The spinner again until the system is up. Plymouth quits just before greetd starts; the greeter’s compositor starts black.
