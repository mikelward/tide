Launcher

Tap Super on its own, or press Super+Space. The launcher opens centered near the top of the focused monitor and dims everything behind it. Type to fuzzy-search app entries, their desktop actions (such as “New Incognito Window”) and built-in quick actions. Esc or a second tap of Super closes it.
