Login and lock

One screen, drawn by the same QML component, serves as the greetd greeter at boot and the session lock (ext-session-lock-v1) afterwards, so you meet the same face either way. It leads with the machine’s short hostname, big: first label, with any leading <user>- removed, the rule i3statusdwm already uses. Local’s date and time sit below it; the zone clocks stay on the bar.
