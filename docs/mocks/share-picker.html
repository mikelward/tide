Screen-share picker

When an app (Chrome, for Meet) asks the portal to share, xdg-desktop-portal-hyprland runs tide’s picker instead of its own. One dialog lists screens, windows and areas. Enter shares the highlighted choice, and arrow keys move through them. While anything is being shared, the bar shows a red Sharing pill; while a screen or area is shared, popups are held too.

Why an “Area” option: a whole 3440×1440 ultrawide shared to Meet arrives letterboxed and unreadable. A 16:9 slice of it (or a single window) fills viewers’ screens. Reuse grants a portal restore token (the r flag), for apps that ask to persist. Chrome opens several portal sessions for one share, so a repeat request within 10 s opens with the last choice preselected: one Enter, never a silent reuse. The portal doesn’t say which app is asking, so the title stays generic. Ultrawide default: the focused window is preselected, not the whole screen.
