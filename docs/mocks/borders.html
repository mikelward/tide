Borders and shadows

The Borders page's Shadows choices, and a border, each on the same desktop at its real size: two tiles side by side and one floating window over them, corners rounded by 6 px. The left tile has the focus; the other two windows are dimmed by 12%. Beside each, a 4× close-up of where the two tiles meet the floating window's top edge.

dimmed: not focused a press here starts a resize (the last close-ups only; nothing on screen shows it)

How they're drawn. As Hyprland 0.57.1 draws them. Colors are theme/palette.json's. A shadow is black at 27% (rgba(00000044)) at the window's edge, falling to nothing over its 8 px with render_power = 2, around the rounded corner. Hyprland draws the tiles in the order they opened with the focused one last, then the floating windows, and the bar above them all: so the focused tile's shadow falls on the tile beside it, a tile's shadow is hidden where a later tile covers it, and no shadow shows on the bar. Where a resize starts is the same whatever the shadows: the 8 px outside a floating window, and 4 px either side of where the tiles meet.
